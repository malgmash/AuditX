# AuditX

**Your AI finance investigator for small businesses.**

---

## What it is

AuditX is a financial investigation platform that helps small businesses find suspicious activity across employee expenses, receipts, invoices, and timesheets.

Instead of a finance manager reviewing thousands of records by hand, AuditX analyzes the records, finds unusual patterns, groups related anomalies into investigation cases, and explains why each case deserves attention.

AuditX does not decide whether fraud occurred. It shows the pattern and the supporting evidence, and a human reviewer makes the final decision.

---

## What it does

**It reads the records.** Expenses, receipt images, invoices, and timesheets are analyzed as they come in.

**It finds what is unusual.** Duplicate receipts, abnormal spending, inconsistent timesheets, and signals across records that are hard to spot by hand, such as hours logged in one city while a receipt places the same person somewhere else on the same day.

**It builds cases.** Related anomalies are grouped into one investigation case, not a set of loose alerts.

**It writes the investigation brief.** For every case, AuditX explains what was found, why it stands out, what evidence supports it, which review steps to take, which questions to ask, and which innocent explanations are plausible.

**It leaves the decision to you.** The reviewer can accept, decline, or look further into the employee's full record and timeline.

---

## For administrators

Sign in to a ranked queue of investigation cases instead of raw transactions. Open a case to see the written brief, the attached evidence, and an explanation of the pattern. Decide, and the decision is recorded.

---

## For employees

Employees can see their own record: what was flagged, why it was flagged, and the status of anything under review. They can also submit expenses and timesheets.
